Standard Customer Journey

Overview: This journey maps the typical lifecycle of a client from initial inquiry through post-trip follow-up, with standard timelines and responsibilities.

1 Discovery

  • New Client Inquiry Platforms: Outlook, Calendly
  • Discovery Call Email Within 30 minutes of inquiry
  • Discovery Call Within 1-2 weeks via Calendly
  • Service Fee Invoice Within 48 hours of call
  • CRM & Itinerary Setup Within 24 hours of confirmation
  • Supplier Outreach 24-48 hours after call

2 Proposal

  • Quote Received 1-2 weeks from suppliers
  • Create Proposal 3-5 days after quote (usually 1-2 days)
  • Send Proposal Via Travefy
  • Travel Insurance Quote Optional, discuss with client
  • Proposal Revisions As needed per client
  • Approval Client approval via Travefy or email

3 Booking

  • Initial Bookings 1-3 days after proposal approval
  • Formal Itinerary 1-5 days after bookings
  • Second Round Bookings 1-5 months after initial
  • Registration Tasks Rewards programs, visas, essentials

4 Destination

  • Destination Email #1 7-5 months before trip
  • Destination Email #2 4-3 months before trip
  • Packing List & Tips Sent with affiliate links

5 Pre-Trip

  • Confirm with Suppliers 105 days before trip
  • Send Itinerary & Form 90 days before trip
  • Final Paperwork 20 days before trip
  • Final Itinerary 14 days before trip
  • Check-In & Bon Voyage 24-48 hours before trip

6 Traveling

  • Arrival Check-In 2 days after trip starts (optional)
  • On-Trip Support Available as needed
  • Rebooking Encouragement For repeat destinations (Sandals, Cruises)

7 Post-Trip

  • Welcome Home Email 5-7 days after trip ends
  • Feedback Survey With review links & referral program
  • Future Trip Follow-Up #1 30-45 days after trip
  • Future Trip Follow-Up #2 80-90 days after trip
  • 6-Month Check-In 180 days after trip

Tern Workflow Integration

Overview: Tern workflows automate and structure the customer journey with predefined tasks, automations, and communication templates. Four main workflows guide the complete trip lifecycle.

Starter Lead Workflow Initial Contact

Triggered when a trip is created in Tern or manually applied. Ensures follow-ups with prospective clients.

  • 00. Lead | Initial Contact Email Send inquiry form AUTO
  • 01. Lead | Initial Contact Follow-Up Resend inquiry form
  • 02. Lead | Schedule Intake Call Send Calendly link
  • 03. Lead | Schedule Intake Call Follow-Up Reminder email
  • 04. Lead | Call Recap, Send Planning Invoice Post-call summary
  • 05. Lead | Upon payment, update status to Planning Activates Planning Workflow AUTO
Automations:
  • Tern Access - 2 days after trip status change to Planning
  • Planning Fee Payment Confirmation - Immediately upon status change

Starter Planning Workflow Proposal Creation

Triggered when trip status changes to PLANNING. Manages proposal creation, revisions, and approval.

  • 00. Planning | Contact DMC / Preferred Suppliers Due: 4 days after task creation
  • 01. Planning | Create & Send Client Proposal Email Due: 7 days after task creation AUTO
  • 02. Planning | Proposal Follow-Up #1 & #2 Due: 20 days after task creation
  • 03. Planning | Proposal Updates (if necessary) Due: 14 days after task creation
  • 04. Planning | Confirm booking(s) with Supplier(s) Due: 7 days after task creation AUTO
  • 05. Planning | Upon Approval, update status to Booked Add trip tags (cruise, safari, VIP, etc.) AUTO
Automations:
  • Proposal Approved - Upon client approval
  • Travel Insurance - 2 days after status change to Planning

Starter Payments Workflow Payment Processing

Automates payment reminders and confirmations throughout the booking process.

Key Automations:
  • Payment Due Reminder 10 Days 10 days before due date + creates task 5 days after
  • Payment Due Reminder 1 Day 1 day before due date + creates task
  • Payment Past Due 1 day after due date + creates follow-up task

Starter Pre/Post Travel Workflow Trip Execution

Triggered when trip status changes to BOOKED. Manages all pre-trip preparation and post-trip follow-up.

  • 00. Request Final Paperwork from Suppliers 35 days before trip start
  • 01. Pre-Travel Documents & Wrap-Up 22 days before trip start AUTO
  • 02. Send Hotel Booking Confirmations 7 days before trip start
  • 03. Submit Commission Notes 5 days before trip start
  • 04. Review Trip Feedback 10 days after trip end AUTO
  • 05. Archive Trip 181 days after trip end
Key Automations:
  • 90-Day Check-In AUTO
  • 45-Day Check-In & Profile Refresh AUTO
  • 24-Hour Flight Check-In Reminder AUTO
  • Arrival Day Check-In AUTO
  • Welcome Home Survey AUTO

Advisor Responsibilities by Phase

Discovery Phase

Who: Advisor, VA, Other

Key Actions:

  • Receive inquiry (Outlook, Calendly, Phone)
  • Send discovery call email within 30 min
  • Conduct discovery call within 1-2 weeks
  • Send service fee invoice within 48 hours
  • Research & outreach to suppliers (24-48 hours)

Proposal Phase

Who: Advisor, VA, Supplier

Key Actions:

  • Wait for supplier quotes (1-2 weeks)
  • Create proposal (3-5 days, typically 1-2)
  • Send proposal to client via Travefy
  • Follow up on proposals (automated or manual)
  • Revise as needed per client feedback
  • Wait for client approval

Booking Phase

Who: Advisor, Supplier, VA, Client

Key Actions:

  • Process credit card authorization
  • Book flights, hotels, activities (1-3 days after approval)
  • Create formal itinerary (1-5 days)
  • Handle second round bookings (1-5 months)
  • Register for loyalty programs & visas

Pre-Trip Phase

Who: Advisor, VA

Key Actions:

  • Confirm with suppliers (105 days before)
  • Send special requests form (90 days before)
  • Send destination emails (7-4 months before)
  • Request final paperwork (20 days before)
  • Send final itinerary (14 days before)
  • Bon voyage check-in (24-48 hours before)

Post-Trip Phase

Who: Advisor, VA

Key Actions:

  • Welcome home email (5-7 days after)
  • Collect feedback & surveys
  • Future trip follow-up #1 (30-45 days)
  • Future trip follow-up #2 (80-90 days)
  • 6-month check-in (180 days)
  • Referral program engagement
